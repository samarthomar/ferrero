# Ferrero AI Procurement Intelligence — Official Demo Script

> **Demo Strategy:** Do not just read the numbers on the screen. Tell a story about **risk, margin protection, and proactive intelligence**. Your goal is to show how this platform shifts Ferrero from *reactive* procurement to *predictive* strategy.

---

## 🖥️ Screen 1: Executive Dashboard (The Command Center)
**Goal:** Establish trust in the data and show total visibility.

**What to say:**
> *"Welcome to the Executive Dashboard. This is the command center for Ferrero’s global hazelnut procurement. What you are looking at is a unified, real-time view of our massive footprint—representing approximately 25% of the world's hazelnut supply.*
>
> *At the top, we have our live KPIs. We aren't just tracking the current weighted-average price of **€9.24/kg**; the engine is calculating our exact **Total Exposure (€319.77M)** based on live warehouse inventory and current market values.*
>
> *We also instantly see our operational health: **50 Days of Supply** and our **Contract Coverage at 45%**. Instead of waiting for end-of-month reports, procurement directors can look at the **Risk Radar** and instantly see that our current biggest threat vector is FX volatility, allowing them to take immediate hedging actions."*

---

## 🌤️ Screen 2: Weather Intelligence (The Risk Predictor)
**Goal:** Show deep agricultural domain knowledge and proactive risk management.

**What to say:**
> *"Because agriculture is entirely dependent on climate, we built a dedicated Weather Intelligence module. This isn't just a weather app—it's an agricultural risk predictor.*
>
> *At the top, we track real-time **Crop Health** and **Chilling Hours** across our three main origins: Turkey, Italy, and Oregon. Hazelnuts need specific chilling hours to break dormancy, and this system tracks that progress.*
>
> *But the real power is historical context. Look at the **Growing Season Timeline** and the **Historical Frost Events**. In April 2025, a massive late frost in Turkey destroyed 50% of the Ordu crop, causing prices to spike 35% in six weeks. This platform is designed to detect the temperature anomalies that cause those events **before** the market reacts, giving Ferrero a massive competitive buying advantage."*

---

## 🌍 Screen 3: Market Intelligence (The Information Hub)
**Goal:** Demonstrate how the system filters global noise into actionable signals.

**What to say:**
> *"Procurement teams are constantly overwhelmed by news, rumors, and disparate data. The Market Intelligence screen acts as an AI-driven filter.*
>
> *At the top, we track the spread between Turkish, Italian, and Oregon origins. Below that, the system generates **Live Signals**. For example, it’s currently flagging **Trabzon Port Congestion** and a pending **TMO Floor Price** announcement.*
>
> *On the right, we have AI-summarized **Intelligence Reports**. Instead of reading 40-page PDFs from analysts, the system extracts the exact supply chain risks and harvest progress notes that matter to Ferrero, summarizing the 'Key Takeaways' instantly."*

---

## 🎛️ Screen 4: Scenario Simulator (The Stress Test)
**Goal:** Show the financial modeling power of the platform.

**What to say:**
> *"This is where the platform moves from tracking data to modeling the future. The Scenario Simulator allows us to stress-test our supply chain against geopolitical and environmental shocks.*
>
> *Let’s say we want to know what happens if that 2025 Turkish Frost happens again. We select the **'2025 Turkey Frost Replay'** preset. The engine automatically adjusts supply disruption to 40% and weather impact to -45%.* 
> 
> *Instantly, the engine calculates the exact financial impact: how many millions of Euros our cost will increase, and exactly how many percentage points our final product gross margin will drop. It then generates a recommended action—like 'Accelerate Procurement'—to mitigate that exact scenario."*

---

## 🤖 Screen 5: AI Procurement Advisor (The Copilot)
**Goal:** Prove the system understands Ferrero's specific B2B business model.

**What to say:**
> *"Finally, we have the AI Procurement Advisor. This isn't a generic chatbot; it is deeply integrated with our live data streams and understands Ferrero HCo's specific business model.*
>
> *If I ask it about **weather risks**, it analyzes the chilling hours and crop health indices we saw earlier to recommend hedging strategies.* 
>
> *More importantly, it understands that Ferrero doesn't just buy hazelnuts; **Ferrero HCo processes and sells them B2B**. If I ask it to 'Analyze Sell-Side Margins,' it calculates the spread between our raw acquisition cost (e.g., €6.50/kg) and the current processed market price (e.g., €11.00/kg). It advises our sales team exactly when to hold inventory for Nutella production, and when to sell processed paste to the open market for maximum profit."*
