May 2026 Supply Chain Disruption Risks:
As we look toward the 2026 harvest, procurement intelligence must factor in elevated logistics and operational risks. Road freight capacity from Turkey to the EU is currently constrained by new border inspection protocols, adding 2-3 days to typical transit times and pushing logistics costs higher. Furthermore, labor shortages for the upcoming harvest season in both Italy and Turkey remain a pressing concern, potentially delaying the availability of new crop materials in September. On a positive note, the crop development looks excellent so far, with a high yield forecast mitigating some of the supply chain gloom. Prices are currently trending upwards steadily, largely driven by these friction costs rather than raw material scarcity.
