February 2025 EU Trade Policy Changes:
The European Commission's recent discussions regarding agricultural import tariffs could have significant implications for hazelnut procurement. While Turkish imports currently enjoy favorable rates under existing customs union agreements, new sustainability documentation requirements are acting as a non-tariff barrier. Suppliers without full traceability down to the farm level are finding it increasingly difficult to clear EU customs smoothly. This is creating a two-tier market where fully certified, traceable hazelnuts command a €0.30/kg premium. Conversely, tariffs on US-origin hazelnuts have been slightly reduced, making Oregon an increasingly attractive alternative for standard industrial grades. Procurement strategies should pivot towards securing fully compliant supply chains ahead of the strict enforcement date.
