October 2025 Competitive Landscape:
The hazelnut processing sector is undergoing rapid consolidation. This month's acquisition of two mid-sized Turkish cracking facilities by a major multinational trading house signals a shift towards tighter vertical integration. For large buyers, this means fewer independent suppliers and potentially reduced price competition at the origin. However, it also promises better quality control and easier compliance with EU sustainability mandates. In response, independent farmers are forming stronger cooperatives to negotiate better prices. The competitive dynamics are shifting from pure price-based procurement to securing reliable, traceable, and sustainable supply. Buyers should focus on long-term partnerships rather than purely transactional spot purchases in this evolving environment.
