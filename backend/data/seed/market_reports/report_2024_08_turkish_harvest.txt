August 2024 Harvest Outlook:
The Turkish harvest has officially begun, and initial reports from the field are mixed. While the Ordu and Giresun regions are reporting slightly lower than average sizes (mostly 11-13mm rather than the preferred 13-15mm), the overall tonnage seems largely in line with pre-season expectations of around 720,000 MT. The TMO (Turkish Grain Board) has announced a purchasing price that provides a solid floor for the market, preventing any significant downward pressure. However, the continuous depreciation of the Turkish Lira is causing some confusion in EUR/USD pricing. Farmers are holding onto their crop as an inflation hedge, which might tighten physical availability in September. In Italy, the Piedmont harvest looks promising with exceptional quality noted.
