November 2024 Global Supply-Demand Balance:
As we move past the peak harvest months, the global hazelnut balance sheet is coming into focus. Demand from major confectioners remains robust, heavily driven by the pre-Christmas manufacturing rush. Specifically, demand for premium Italian Tonda Gentile is outstripping supply, pushing prices towards the €10.50/kg mark. Turkish exports are flowing steadily, though logistics costs from the Black Sea to Northern Europe have seen a 10% increase compared to last year, squeezing margins. Global ending stocks for the 2024/25 season are projected to be historically tight (down 5% YoY). We advise procurement teams to finalize Q1 2025 contracts promptly, as any supply chain disruption could leave late buyers exposed to spot market volatility.
