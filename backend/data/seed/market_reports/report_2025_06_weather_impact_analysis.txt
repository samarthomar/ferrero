June 2025 Weather Impact Analysis:
A prolonged dry spell across the Mediterranean basin is causing alarm among agronomists. Both the Black Sea coast of Turkey and the Piedmont region in Italy have received 40% less rainfall than the 10-year average for May-June. Soil moisture levels are critically low during the crucial nut-fill stage. Our models indicate that the crop health index for Turkey has dropped below 80, suggesting a potential 5-8% reduction in overall yield. As expected, market reaction has been swift, with futures contracts for September delivery jumping 8% in the past two weeks. If significant rainfall does not arrive by early July, we may see a fundamental shift in the supply curve, with Turkish prices potentially testing the €8.50/kg barrier.
