March 2024 Market Update:
Temperatures in the Black Sea region have plummeted this week, raising concerns about potential frost damage to the developing hazelnut buds. While it is still too early to quantify the exact impact on crop health, historical precedents suggest that sustained temperatures below -2°C at this stage could reduce yields by 10-15%. Consequently, spot prices for Turkish hazelnuts have shown a noticeable uptick in the last few days, breaking the €7.50/kg resistance line. Buyers who remain unhedged for Q3 are advised to consider locking in some volumes, as any confirmed frost damage could trigger a massive rally. Meanwhile, Oregon orchards are reporting normal chill hours and excellent catkin development, pointing to a potentially record crop if conditions hold.
