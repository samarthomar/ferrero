# Executive Dashboard: Exact Mathematical Breakdown

This document traces the exact calculation of every number shown on the **Executive Dashboard** (based on your screenshot), proving exactly how the Python backend derives them from the raw data.

---

## 1. CURRENT PRICE (9.24 €/kg)
**What it is:** The global weighted average price of raw hazelnut kernels on the latest recorded day.
**How it is calculated:** 
The backend pulls the latest daily prices for all three origins (Turkey, Italy, Oregon) from `hazelnut_prices.csv` and applies a weighted average based on the trading volume of that day.
* **Formula:** `Sum of (Price × Volume for each origin) ÷ Total Daily Volume`
* **Example from data:** 
  * Turkey: 9.30 €/kg at 2,202 tons
  * Italy: 9.20 €/kg at 241 tons
  * Oregon: 8.18 €/kg at 117 tons
  * **Result:** The weighted average correctly pulls to **9.24 €/kg** because Turkey dominates the volume.

---

## 2. PRICE TREND (-0.99% vs last month)
**What it is:** The percentage change in the Current Price over the last 30 days.
**How it is calculated:**
* **Formula:** `((Current Price - Price 30 Days Ago) ÷ Price 30 Days Ago) × 100`
* **Math:** `((9.24 - 9.33) ÷ 9.33) × 100 = -0.99%`

---

## 3. TOTAL EXPOSURE (319.77 €M)
**What it is:** The total financial value of all hazelnuts currently sitting in Ferrero's warehouses if valued at today's market price.
**How it is calculated:**
* **Data point:** Total inventory across all warehouses (Turkey + Italy + Germany) on this day = **34,607 tons**.
* **Formula:** `(Total Inventory Tons × 1000 to get kg) × Current Price €/kg`
* **Math:** `34,607 tons × 1000 = 34,607,000 kg`
* `34,607,000 kg × 9.24 €/kg = €319,768,680`
* **Result displayed:** Divided by 1 million and rounded to two decimals = **319.77 €M**

---

## 4. DAYS OF SUPPLY (50)
**What it is:** How many days Ferrero's factories can keep running on current inventory before running out.
**How it is calculated:**
* **Data point:** Ferrero consumes approximately **250,000 tons per year**, which equals **~685 tons per day**.
* **Formula:** `Total Inventory Tons ÷ Daily Consumption`
* **Math:** `34,607 tons ÷ 685 tons/day = 50.5 days`
* **Result displayed:** **50** (Rounded to whole number)

---

## 5. INVENTORY UTIL. (76.9%)
**What it is:** How full Ferrero's global warehouses are currently.
**How it is calculated:**
* **Data point:** The maximum combined physical capacity of Ferrero's warehouses (Ordu, Alba, Frankfurt) in the system is set to **45,000 tons**.
* **Formula:** `(Total Inventory Tons ÷ Max Capacity) × 100`
* **Math:** `(34,607 ÷ 45,000) × 100 = 76.904%`
* **Result displayed:** **76.9%**

---

## 6. ACTIVE CONTRACTS (7)
**What it is:** The total number of active supplier agreements.
**How it is calculated:**
* The backend reads `supplier_contracts.json` and runs a simple count function.
* **Formula:** `Count of records where status == "Active"`
* **Result:** **7** (Olam, Balsu, Progida, Özstar, Sagra, Ferrero Agrifarming, Oregon Hazelnut Co.)

---

## 7. CONTRACT COVERAGE (45%)
**What it is:** The percentage of Ferrero's total annual demand that is already locked in via active contracts.
**How it is calculated:**
* **Data point:** Annual demand is 250,000 tons. The sum of locked volumes in the 7 active contracts is **112,500 tons**.
* **Formula:** `(Total Contracted Volume ÷ Annual Demand) × 100`
* **Math:** `(112,500 ÷ 250,000) × 100 = 45.0%`
* **Result displayed:** **45%**

---

## 8. FX RATES (EUR/USD: 1.1054 | EUR/TRY: 42.41)
**What it is:** Current exchange rates critical to procurement.
**How it is calculated:**
* There is no math here; these are raw data pulls. The backend fetches the latest row from `exchange_rates.csv` for the current simulated day (September 2026).
* The **42.41** TRY rate accurately reflects the simulated inflation/depreciation curve of the Turkish Lira up to late 2026.

---

## 9. RISK RADAR (The Spider Chart)
**What it is:** A 0-100 score on six risk dimensions. 100 is maximum danger.
**How they are calculated:**
1. **Supply:** `100 - Days of Supply`. Math: `100 - 50 = 50`.
2. **Weather:** `100 - Crop Health Index`. If crop health is 75, risk is 25.
3. **FX (Currency):** Calculated using the standard deviation of EUR/TRY daily percentage changes over the last 30 days. Higher volatility = higher risk score.
4. **Logistics:** Directly correlated to 7-day price momentum. (Rapid price spikes usually indicate logistical scrambles at ports).
5. **Demand:** Based on the 30-day slope of Ferrero product demand forecasts.
6. **Tariff:** Fixed at 35 (moderate) as EU-Turkey agricultural tariffs are generally stable but require monitoring.

---

## 10. CRITICAL ALERTS
**What it is:** Real-time event triggers generated by the Market Intel module.
**How they are calculated:**
The backend (`market_intel.py`) constantly scans the data streams for anomalies.
* **Trabzon Port Congestion:** Triggered dynamically as a logistical flag.
* **Frost-Price Correlation:** This is an AI-generated insight based on historical analysis (noting that April frost events historically lead to 22% average price spikes within 60 days).
